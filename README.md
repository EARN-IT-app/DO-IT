# DO IT

A free website that stops you procrastinating. When you open a distracting app, your iPhone sends you to DO IT first. You get up, take a photo that proves you're working (your homework, your desk, a glass of water, the sky), and only then do you earn a short break.

- The photo check runs on your phone with TensorFlow.js, so photos never leave the phone.
- To choose which apps send you here, use a Shortcuts automation ("App is opened" → "Open URLs"). The steps are on the page itself.

Free to use and share.
