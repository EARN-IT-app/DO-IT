Pick the apps to block

You choose the apps in your iPhone's Shortcuts app. Do this once:

  1. Open the Shortcuts app. Tap Automation at the bottom, then the + button.
  2. Tap App, then Choose. Pick the apps that distract you, like TikTok or Instagram. Tap Done.
  3. Choose Run Immediately and tap Next.
  4. Tap New Blank Automation, then Add Action. Search for Open URLs and tap it.
  5. Tap the blue word URL, paste the DO IT address, and tap Done.
Open Shortcuts

Now whenever you open one of those apps, DO IT opens first and takes the time from today's 30 minutes.
